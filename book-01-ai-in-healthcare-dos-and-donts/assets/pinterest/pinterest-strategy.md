## 7. Pinterest SEO Tips

### Keywords to use in every pin:
```
ai in healthcare, healthcare ai, hospital ai, ai implementation,
data privacy, hipaa, gdpr, dpdp act, business analyst,
healthcare technology, digital health, hospital management,
ai ethics, patient safety, medical technology
```

### Where to put keywords:
1. **Pin title** — first 40 characters (most visible)
2. **Pin description** — first 100 characters (shows in feed)
3. **Board name** — exact match to search terms
4. **Board description** — repeat keywords naturally
5. **Profile bio** — include main keywords

### Pinterest Algorithm Tips:
- **Consistency** > volume (5 pins/day beats 50 on Monday then nothing)
- **Fresh pins** beat re-pins (create new designs, same content)
- **Vertical format** gets more reach than horizontal
- **Text on pins** gets more clicks than image-only
- **Link every pin** — always include the Gumroad URL
- **Engage** — follow accounts in your niche, repin relevant content
