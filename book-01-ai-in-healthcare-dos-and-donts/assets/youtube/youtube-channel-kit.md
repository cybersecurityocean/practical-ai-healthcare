## 7. Video Script — #1: "AI in Healthcare: 15 Do's & Don'ts"

**Hook (0–15s):**
> Hospitals are being told to "do AI" right now. Most of them will waste money and some will hurt patients. Not because AI is dangerous — but because almost nobody tells you the do's and don'ts. That's exactly this video. Let's go.

**Do's (value: outline the 6):**
> 1. Define the problem BEFORE you pick the tool.
> 2. Involve clinicians and stakeholders early.
> 3. Validate on YOUR data, not the vendor's demo.
> 4. Keep a human in the loop — AI recommends, humans decide.
> 5. Document everything. Model cards, audit trails.
> 6. Monitor after deployment — AI degrades silently.

**Don'ts (outline the 5):**
> 1. Don't trust vendor hype or "AI-washed" products.
> 2. Don't touch patient data without protection (DPDP, HIPAA).
> 3. Don't deploy without a failure plan.
> 4. Don't ignore bias.
> 5. Don't skip explainability.

**Close (last 20s):**
> If any of these hit home, I wrote the complete guide — all 50 checklist points, HIPAA/GDPR/DPDP explained, case studies. It's free, link is below. Subscribe for practical healthcare AI, not hype.

**Where to pitch:** description (line 1), pinned comment, end-screen card.

## 8. Video Script — #2: "Will AI Replace Doctors? The Honest Answer"

**Hook:**
> "AI will replace doctors" is the most profitable lie in healthcare media right now. The honest answer is more useful — and more hopeful. Here it is.

**Body:**
> - What AI actually does today (pattern recognition, prediction, automation — not reasoning).
> - The 3 categories: Replaced (some administrative tasks), Redefined (most clinical roles), Untouched (judgment, accountability, empathy, bedside care).
> - Why doctors won't be replaced: accountability is a legal, human thing — machines can't own it.
> - The real shift: every clinician becomes an AI-supervisor.
> - What the evidence shows: tools augment, they don't supplant; failures happen when the human leaves the loop.

**Close:**

## 9. Video Script — #3: "Internal Server or Cloud for Hospital AI?"

**Hook:**
> Every hospital asks the same question: should our AI live on an internal server or in the cloud? The right answer isn't "cloud is better" — it's "it depends on these 5 things."

**Body:**
> 1. Data residency — where can patient data legally live? (DPDP cross-border rules)
> 2. Security — what each option actually protects.
> 3. Cost — CAPEX (buy servers) vs OPEX (pay monthly) + hidden costs.
> 4. Performance — latency for real-time decisions vs batch jobs.
> 5. Control — who holds the keys, who can move the data.
> Hybrid is often the answer: sensitive data on-premise, compute in cloud.

**Close:**
> The full decision framework — 15 questions and a scorecard — is in the series. Link below.
