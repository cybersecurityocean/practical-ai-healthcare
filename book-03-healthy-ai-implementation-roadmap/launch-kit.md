## LinkedIn Posts

### Post 1: The Problem

85% of AI projects never make it to production.

Not because the algorithms are bad.
Not because the compute is unavailable.
Not because the data does not exist.

Because the roadmap does not exist.

Hospitals start AI projects without governance. They select projects based on vendor demos instead of clinical needs. They skip data readiness. They ignore change management.

And when the pilot does not produce magical results, they abandon the project.

My new book, **Healthy AI Implementation Roadmap for Hospitals**, is the step-by-step playbook that fixes this.

### Post 2: The Selection Problem

Every vendor claims their AI product will transform care. Every conference features success stories. Every article promises revolution.

The result: hospitals pick projects based on excitement instead of evidence.

My new book introduces the **Project Selection Matrix** — a weighted framework that scores potential AI projects on three dimensions:
- Clinical Need (40%)
- Feasibility (35%)
- ROI Potential (25%)

For every project you approve, you should reject or defer at least two. This is not pessimism — it is focus.

### Post 3: The Governance Rule

The single most effective AI governance mechanism is simple:

**No AI project can proceed without Steering Committee approval. No exceptions.**

This eliminates the "shadow AI" problem — where departments run AI projects without organizational oversight, creating compliance risks and duplicating effort.

My new book, **Healthy AI Implementation Roadmap for Hospitals**, explains how to build the governance structure that makes AI projects accountable.

Link: [LINK]

### Post 4: The 80/20 Rule

AI implementation is 20% technology and 80% people.

The best AI solution is worthless if clinicians do not use it.

My new book covers change management for healthcare AI:
- How to identify clinical champions
- How to communicate the "why" (hint: frame it in patient outcomes)
- How to address the fear of replacement
- How to provide just-in-time training
- How to celebrate wins

Link: [LINK]

### Post 5: The 12-Month Roadmap

My new book includes a printable 12-month implementation roadmap:

Month 1-2: Foundation (Governance, readiness, candidates)
Month 3-4: Selection (Matrix scoring, vendor evaluation)
Month 5-6: Preparation (Data remediation, integration, training)
Month 7-9: Pilot (Launch, monitor, collect feedback)
Month 10: Evaluation (ROI calculation, scaling decision)
Month 11-12: Scale or Terminate

Print it. Use it. Track progress.

### Video 1: "Why 85% of AI Projects Fail in Hospitals"

**Duration:** 5-7 minutes

[INTRO]
85% of AI projects never make it to production. This is not a technology problem. It is an organizational problem.

[THE PATTERN]
Every failed AI project follows the same pattern:
1. Excitement — leadership reads an article or hears a vendor pitch
2. Selection — someone picks the project with the best demo
3. Pilot — it shows promising results on a limited dataset
4. Expansion — leadership wants to scale
5. Friction — clinicians resist, data issues emerge, integration is hard

2. Wrong project selection
3. Data not ready
4. No change management
5. No measurement
